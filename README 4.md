**Author:** Mrs. Mariyah Aziz
**Format:** Paperback (Live on Amazon) | Kindle eBook
**Platform:** Amazon KDP (Self-Published)

## Overview

*Overthinking Journal for Women 25–40* is a guided, structured journal designed to help
women in their 20s and 30s manage racing thoughts, emotional overwhelm, and mental
spirals. Through targeted prompts and a guided emotional reset framework, the journal
helps readers slow down, process their thoughts, and regain a sense of calm and clarity
in daily life.

The journal blends practical self-reflection exercises with a calming, supportive tone,
making it accessible for readers seeking an actionable, non-clinical tool for everyday
emotional regulation.
